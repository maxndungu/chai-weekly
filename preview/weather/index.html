Rain and frost watch for the tea areas

Rain over the past seven days and the forecast for the next seven, for the main tea-growing areas of Kenya and the region. Tea likes steady rain and warm nights; a heavy downpour, a dry week or a cold night all show up in the leaf and at the auction a few weeks later.
