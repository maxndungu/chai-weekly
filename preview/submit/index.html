Contact

Write to Chai Weekly

Something happening at your factory, on your shamba, at the auction or in your tea cup? Tell us. Good stories go into the Wednesday edition, with your name if you want it. You can also use this form for letters, corrections and any other question.

So we can reply and check details. Never published.

What we look for

  • News from your factory or county: payments, bonuses, new machines, elections, disputes.
  • What you see at the auction or in the market.
  • Farming that worked (or didn't): pruning, fertiliser, pests, new varieties.
  • Tea culture: your family's chai, a tea shop, a festival, a recipe.
  • Photos of the season: plucking, the factory, the rain (or lack of it).

How it works

We read everything. We check facts before publishing, and we may contact you first. We edit for length and clarity. Not every story is published, and we can't pay for submissions. Nothing you send appears on the site automatically.

Please be fair: no personal attacks, and use people's proper titles.

You can also reply to any Chai Weekly email.
