Data and sources

Made tea is tea after processing, as it is sold. It is the figure the tea trade uses. Fresh green leaf weighs about four to five times as much.

1961 to 2020: national production as reported to the United Nations, for every country that grows tea commercially. Countries that have since split or merged are shown as they were reported, such as the USSR to 1991.

2021 on: national figures where they are published (tea boards, statistics offices and industry associations), and industry statistics for the other largest producers. They can differ a little in method from the earlier figures. Other countries are added as their national figures are gathered.

Area harvested is the land under tea that was picked that year.

Production 1961 to 2020 and area: FAO, FAOSTAT, CC BY 4.0. Figures are as published by each source. Spot an error? Just reply to any issue.
