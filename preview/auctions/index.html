Data and sources

Weekly sales: East African Tea Trade Association auction statistics, all teas sold at each sale, with CTC and orthodox shown separately where EATTA reports them. EATTA's site only shows the current year, so Chai Weekly keeps each sale as it is published and updates it if EATTA revises a figure. Sale averages for earlier years in the year-by-year table come from Chai Weekly's own weekly auction records, back to 2009; before that, the year's World Bank monthly prices are averaged.

Monthly series since 1992: the Mombasa auction price for each month. It runs a few cents above EATTA's own sale averages because the two are measured differently, so the page shows them side by side rather than mixing them.

Monthly prices since 1992: World Bank Commodity Price Data, CC BY 4.0. Figures are as published by each source. Check the originals before trading on them. Spot an error? Just reply to any issue.
