Mombasa Tea Auction

The world's largest auction for black CTC tea. Teas from Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, Ethiopia, DR Congo, Malawi, Madagascar and Mozambique are sold here in US dollars.

More tea, same price at Mombasa

Buyers took 7.51 million kg at Sale 46, up 10% on the week, and the average held at $2.35 a kilo. Mununga's tea topped KTDA prices at $3.38.
