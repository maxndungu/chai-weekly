Data and sources

Weekly sales: East African Tea Trade Association auction statistics, all teas sold at each sale, with CTC and orthodox shown separately where EATTA reports them. EATTA's site only shows the current year, so Chai Weekly keeps each sale as it is published and updates it if EATTA revises a figure. Sales from 2009 to 2025 come from Chai Weekly's own weekly auction records.

1992 to 2008: the monthly average Mombasa price, the figure available for those years. It is measured a little differently from a sale average, so the change on the year is not shown between 2008 and 2009.

Monthly prices 1992 to 2008: World Bank Commodity Price Data, CC BY 4.0. Figures are as published by each source. Check the originals before trading on them. Spot an error? Just reply to any issue.
