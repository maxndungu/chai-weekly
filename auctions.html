Weekly sales: East African Tea Trade Association auction statistics, all teas sold at each sale, with CTC and orthodox shown separately where EATTA reports them. EATTA's site only shows the current year, so Chai Weekly keeps each sale as it is published and updates it if EATTA revises a figure.

Monthly series: World Bank Commodity Price Data (the "Pink Sheet"), "Tea, Mombasa": an average of weekly quotes for all teas of African origin, licensed CC BY 4.0. It runs a few cents above EATTA's own sale averages because the two are measured differently, so the page shows them side by side rather than mixing them.

Figures are as published by each source. Check the originals before trading on them. Spot an error? Just reply to any issue.
