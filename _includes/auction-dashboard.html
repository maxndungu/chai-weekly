    The long view: the Mombasa price since 1992

    US$ per kg, not adjusted for inflation: the monthly average from 1992 to 2008, then the average at every sale from 2009.

    {%- endunless %}

    * Provisional: EATTA adds late sales until Friday noon, Nairobi time, so the newest sale's figures can still change until then.

    The average for a run of sales is weighted by the kilograms sold at each sale. Share sold (absorption) is the kilograms sold as a share of the kilograms offered, both from the same source, and the offered and sold bars use the same figures. EATTA's own count of kilograms sold, used for the headline figure and the table, can differ by a few per cent from the figures behind the share sold. Orthodox tea is sold every other week, so the Orthodox view lists only the weeks it was sold. Separate orthodox figures start at sale 38 of 2025.

    Sources: prices and kilograms sold from 2026 are from the East African Tea Trade Association's auction statistics, updated every Tuesday at 4pm and Friday at 2pm (final figures), Nairobi time.{% unless include.long == false %} Sales from 2009 to 2025 come from Chai Weekly's own weekly auction records.
