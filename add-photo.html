Add a photo

Pick a photo, then drag the frame over the part you want readers to see. Portrait (tall) photos work too: the frame shows exactly what will appear across the top of the page. Nothing here is published; the photo stays on your device until you send it.

1Choose the photo

2Choose the part that shows

Drag the frame (or use the arrow keys) to move it. Zoom in to show a smaller part of the photo.
