### Assam tea and the EU's lower pesticide residue limits (requested 16 Nov 2026) — done in issue #3 (2026-11-18), lead item of Around the tea world
Section: Around the tea world (lead item of that section).
Frame it as **tighter maximum residue limits (MRLs)**, NOT a "ban": the chemicals can still be used, but residues above the limit cannot be sold in the EU.
Checked facts (16 Nov 2026):
- The EU lowered limits for three neonicotinoid insecticides used on tea to the 0.05 ppm default: thiacloprid (from May 2025), and thiamethoxam and clothianidin (from March 2026; EU Regulation 2023/334, applying from 7 March 2026 — confirm on eur-lex). So these limits are now IN FORCE, not upcoming.
- About 40 million kg of Assam tea a year goes to the EU and UK; Germany and the UK take around 20 million kg each (Assam Tribune, 27 Feb 2025).
- Tea Research Association secretary Mr Joydeep Phukan: "The EU notification on lowering MRL for three compounds has become critical." (Assam Tribune, 27 Feb 2025)
- Growers say the alternatives (chlorfenapyr, flupyradifurone) are not yet approved for tea in India (KNN India, 30 May 2025).
- Testing gap: Assam lacks enough residue-testing capacity, especially for its 1.5 lakh+ small growers (about half of Assam's crop), Sentinel Assam editorial, 22 Oct 2026. The same editorial covers India's FSSAI draft limits for acetamiprid (20 mg/kg) and imidacloprid (50 mg/kg), against the EU's 0.05 ppm.
- UK: NOT confirmed. None of the sources say whether Great Britain has adopted the same lower limits (GB sets its own MRLs since Brexit). Do not say the UK limits are the same unless you find a UK government (HSE/GB MRL register) source; otherwise write "exports to Europe" and say the UK position is unclear.
