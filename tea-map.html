Data and sources

Made tea is tea after processing, as it is sold. The map and the blocks run to 2025; figures for 2025 are provisional. See the Production page for how the figures fit together.

Exported is tea sent abroad by the countries that grow it; tea bought in and sold on is not counted again. Imported is tea bought in for drinking. Drunk is production plus imports less exports for tea-growing countries, and imports for the rest; it is left blank where it comes out below zero (Kenya, whose exports include neighbours' tea). The Production page has every country's figures year by year.

Auction prices are each year's average for all teas sold at the auction, weighted by the kilos sold. Every sale is on the Auctions page.

Production and trade 1961 to 2015: FAO, FAOSTAT, CC BY 4.0; 2016 on: International Tea Committee and national tea boards. Map outlines: Natural Earth. Borders are shown as drawn there and imply no view on any boundary.
