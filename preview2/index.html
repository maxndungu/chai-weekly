Markets

Best price of the year at Mombasa

Buyers paid an average of $2.39 a kilo for 7.81 million kg at Sale 47, up 5 cents on the week, on EATTA's provisional figures.

 Industry

A deal to sell Kenyan tea by its name

EATTA and KEPROBA sign a five-year pact to move exports beyond bulk, and the Tea Board tells brokers to build their own brands.

 East Africa

Uganda plans Shs198 billion to revive its tea factories

The Finance Minister told Parliament the government plans Shs152 billion in working capital for factories and a Shs46 billion fertiliser fund.

 World

Sri Lanka's exports dip, but Turkey buys more than double

Sri Lanka earned about $1.08 billion from tea in the first nine months of 2026, down from $1.16 billion, as shipments to Turkey jumped 134%.

 Weather

More rain for 11 tea counties, cold nights on Mt Kenya

Kenya Met's outlook to 30 November puts rain over most tea counties and nights below 10°C in parts of Meru, Nyeri, Kirinyaga and Murang'a.

 Farming

Replanting? Ask about the new clones

Kiegoi growers went to the Tea Research Institute to learn about elite cultivars, nurseries and pest control. You can ask the same questions.

 Spotlight

Browns Plantations bets on orthodox tea

The Kericho and Bomet grower has added a new orthodox line at Changana and is commissioning another at Kimugu this month.

 Culture

Lanterns and tea-tribe games in Assam

The sixth Folk Tea Festival opens at Hatipoti Tea Estate this weekend with music, theatre and tea tasting.
