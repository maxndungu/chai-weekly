Data and sources

Made tea is tea after processing, as it is sold. It is the figure the tea trade uses. Fresh green leaf weighs about four to five times as much.

1961 to 2015: national production as reported to the United Nations, for every country that grows tea commercially. For Iran, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand, Burundi and Mozambique the reported figures from 1991 to 2015 count fresh leaf rather than made tea, so those years are left out for them and taken off the world total. Countries that have since split or merged are shown as they were reported, such as the USSR to 1991.

2016 on: tea industry statistics for every producing country, with national tea board figures in their place where published (Kenya, India and Sri Lanka). They can differ a little in method from the earlier figures. Figures for 2025 are provisional and may be revised.

Area harvested is the land under tea that was picked that year.

Exports are tea sent abroad by the countries that grow it. Tea that a country buys in and sells on (the UK, Germany or the United Arab Emirates, for example) is not counted again. Kenya's exports include tea grown elsewhere in East Africa and sold through Mombasa.

Imports are tea bought in for drinking: for countries that grow no tea, imports less what they sell on; for tea-growing countries, all imports.

Tea drunk is worked out as production plus imports less exports for tea-growing countries, and imports for the rest. It moves with stocks, so a single year can jump. It is left blank where it comes out below zero, as it does for Kenya in most recent years because its exports include other countries' tea.

Trade, 1961 to 2015: national trade figures as reported to the United Nations. 2016 on: tea industry statistics, with United Nations figures for countries they show only in groups.

Production and trade 1961 to 2015, and area: FAO, FAOSTAT, CC BY 4.0. 2016 on: International Tea Committee, with national tea boards for Kenya, India and Sri Lanka. Figures are as published by each source. Spot an error? Just reply to any issue.
