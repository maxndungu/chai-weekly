# About Chai Weekly

A free, non-commercial newsletter for anyone who grows, sells, studies or simply drinks tea. It is rooted in Kenya but follows the whole tea world. Each week: the Mombasa auction in plain numbers, KTDA and factory news, the big stories from other tea countries (India, Sri Lanka, China, Japan, Rwanda, Uganda and more), the weather in Kenya's growing counties, one practical shamba tip, and a bit of tea culture.

Every figure links to its source. Drafts are prepared with AI help from public news and then checked by a human before sending.
