# About Chai Weekly

A free, non-commercial newsletter for anyone who grows, sells, studies or simply drinks tea. It looks at tea from East Africa: the ten countries whose tea is traded through the East African Tea Trade Association and the Mombasa auction (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, Ethiopia, the DR Congo, Malawi, Madagascar and Mozambique), and follows the rest of the tea world from there. Each week: the Mombasa auction in plain numbers, KTDA and factory news, news from the region's other tea countries, the big stories from India, Sri Lanka, China, Japan and beyond, the weather in Kenya's growing counties, one practical shamba tip, and a bit of tea culture.

Every figure links to its source.
